My Story

From freelance generalist to SEO, GEO & AEO specialist

I started the way most independent consultants do — taking on any digital marketing work I could do well. Over time, one discipline kept producing the clearest, most repeatable results for clients: search.

Today I work exclusively on SEO, GEO and AEO for SaaS, eCommerce, healthcare and legal brands — businesses where a single ranking or AI citation can be worth more than a quarter of paid ad spend. I keep my client roster deliberately small so every account gets senior-level attention, not a rotating cast of juniors.

My approach treats search as infrastructure, not a campaign. Technical foundations first, then content and authority, then — increasingly — structuring everything so AI answer engines cite you as the source, not just Google.

Philosophy

Three principles behind every engagement

◆

Technical first, always

Content and links can't compensate for a site Google struggles to crawl, render or trust. Every engagement starts with the technical foundation.

◆

Transparent, plain-language reporting

You'll always know exactly what was done, why, and what it moved — no vanity dashboards dressed up to look busy.

◆

Search is now two channels, not one

Buyers research on Google and on AI engines. Strategy has to account for both, or you're only half-visible.
